Shake Flash Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shake Flash ("the App") handles information when you use its flashlight, shake-detection, and optional Pocket Mode features.

Information the App handles

The App does not require an account. Shake count, cooldown, Pocket Mode, vibration intensity, and whether background detection is enabled are saved locally on your device so your preferences can be remembered. These settings are not sent to a Shake Flash server by the App.

When shake detection is enabled, accelerometer readings are processed on your device to recognize a shake. If Pocket Mode is enabled and your device has a proximity sensor, proximity readings are used on-device to help prevent accidental flashlight activation. These sensor readings are not saved as a history by the App.

Permissions and device features

  • Camera: Used by the operating system to operate the camera flash. The App does not take or store photos or videos.
  • Notifications: Used to show the ongoing notification while background shake detection is running.
  • Motion and proximity sensors: Used locally for shake detection and, when enabled, Pocket Mode. Sensor availability depends on your device.
  • Vibration: Used to provide feedback when the flashlight is toggled, according to your vibration setting.
  • Background service: Runs the detection feature you enable and may be limited by your operating system or device manufacturer.

You can turn off background detection in the App and manage or revoke permissions through your device settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Sharing, analytics, and advertising

The current version of the App does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or Shake Flash backend. The App does not sell personal information. Your operating system, device manufacturer, app store, or any service you choose to use may handle information under its own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Preferences remain in the App's local storage until you change them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Sensor readings are processed for the feature and are not retained as a history by the App. You can stop sensor processing by turning off background detection or uninstalling the App.

Children's privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. The current version does not ask users to submit personal information.

Security

App preferences are stored locally using the platform's application storage. No method of storage or electronic service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with the security features provided by its operating system.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page. Please review the effective date above when checking for updates.

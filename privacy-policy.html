Shake Flash Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shake Flash ("the App") handles information when you use its flashlight, shake detection, Pocket Mode, and device setup guide features.

1. Information the App Handles

Shake Flash does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its features.

The App stores certain preferences locally on your device, including shake count, cooldown duration, Pocket Mode, vibration intensity, and background detection settings. These preferences are used to remember your settings and are not sent to a Shake Flash server.

When shake detection is enabled, accelerometer readings are processed locally on your device to recognize shake gestures. When Pocket Mode is enabled and the required sensor is available, proximity sensor readings are processed locally to help prevent accidental flashlight activation.

The App does not maintain a history of accelerometer or proximity sensor readings.

2. Device Information and Device Settings Guide

Shake Flash may access basic device information using the device_info_plus package, including:

  • Device manufacturer (such as Samsung, Xiaomi, or Huawei).
  • Device brand (such as Redmi, POCO, or Samsung).
  • Device model.

This information is used locally to identify the device manufacturer and display relevant background operation and battery optimization instructions.

The Device Settings Guide may provide manufacturer-specific instructions for configuring settings such as:

  • Battery optimization and unrestricted battery usage.
  • Autostart or automatic launch permissions.
  • Background activity permissions.
  • Recent Apps locking and background execution settings.

The App does not automatically modify these manufacturer-specific settings. Users must manually follow the instructions and apply any changes through their device settings.

Device information is used only to provide the relevant setup guide and is not transmitted to a Shake Flash server by the App.

3. Permissions and Device Features

Shake Flash uses certain Android permissions and device features to provide its functionality:

  • Camera / Flashlight: Used to access the device's camera flash LED as a flashlight. The App does not capture, record, or store photos or videos.
  • Notifications: Used to display an ongoing notification while background shake detection is running.
  • Motion Sensors: Accelerometer readings are used locally to detect shake gestures.
  • Proximity Sensor: Used locally for Pocket Mode when the feature is enabled and the device supports the sensor.
  • Vibration: Used to provide feedback when the flashlight is toggled, according to your vibration settings.
  • Background Service: Used to maintain shake detection in the background when the user enables this feature.
  • Wake Lock: May be used to support background detection while the relevant service is active.

You can disable background detection within the App and manage or revoke available permissions through your Android device settings.

Revoking certain permissions may prevent related features from working correctly.

4. Sharing, Analytics, and Advertising

The current version of Shake Flash does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or a Shake Flash backend server.

The App does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Your Android operating system, device manufacturer, app store, or any external service you independently choose to use may process information under its own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

App preferences are stored locally using the device's application storage.

These preferences remain on your device until you change them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

Sensor readings and basic device information used for the setup guide are processed locally and are not retained as a history by the App.

You can stop background sensor processing by disabling background detection or uninstalling Shake Flash.

6. Children's Privacy

Shake Flash does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to submit personal information or create an account.

7. Data Security

App preferences are stored locally using Android's application storage mechanisms.

Although reasonable measures are used to keep local app data within the device's application storage, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend protecting your device with the security features provided by your operating system.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on Android system features and Flutter packages to provide flashlight, sensor, notification, vibration, and device information functionality.

These features operate through your device's operating system. Any information independently processed by the operating system or other third-party services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the App's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page. Please review the effective date at the top of this policy to check for updates.
